Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 26, 2026  ·  Last Updated: May 26, 2026

Short version: The Toll stores all data locally on your device. We collect nothing. We send nothing. There are no servers, no accounts, and no analytics.

1. Overview

The Toll ("the App," "we," "us," "our") is developed and published by Liu Jinxing. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding your personal information when you use the App on iOS devices.

We believe privacy is a default, not a feature. The App is designed from the ground up to operate entirely on your device.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information.

The App stores the following data exclusively on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework (local on-device storage):

  • Your age and gender (for actuarial estimates)
  • Names, ages, and relationships of family members you enter
  • Daily check-in records (lifestyle level and optional detail flags)
  • App settings and preferences

This data never leaves your device. It is not transmitted to any server, not accessible to us, and not shared with any third party.

3. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • No account registration or login
  • No email address or contact information
  • No location data
  • No health or fitness data (no HealthKit integration)
  • No photos, contacts, or device identifiers
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or usage telemetry
  • No advertising identifiers

4. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics services, advertising networks, or cloud services. The only external system involved is Apple's App Store for distribution and in-app purchase processing.

Apple's handling of purchase data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. In-App Purchase

The App offers a one-time purchase ($0.99) to unlock full functionality. This transaction is processed entirely by Apple. We receive confirmation that a purchase was made, but we do not receive your payment details, Apple ID, or any personal information associated with your Apple account.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

All data is stored locally on your device. You can delete all App data at any time by:

  • Using the Reset function within the App's Settings screen, or
  • Deleting the App from your device (which removes all associated data)

We have no access to your data and cannot delete it on your behalf.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is rated 4+ on the App Store and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Because we collect no personal information from any user, there is no special risk for younger users.

8. Medical Disclaimer

The Toll is a lifestyle awareness and entertainment tool. The "Damage Score" is a motivational metric based on generalized actuarial estimates and publicly available research on lifestyle risk factors. It is not a medical device, does not provide clinical diagnoses, and is not a substitute for professional medical advice.

If you have health concerns, please consult a qualified healthcare professional.

9. Changes to This Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, we will revise the "Last Updated" date above and post the new policy at this URL. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
