Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 27, 2026

1. Summary

The Toll does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on any server. All information you enter — including age, gender, family member details, daily check-ins, and any custom names — lives only on your iOS device.

2. What Data the App Uses

  • Personal profile: age, gender (used locally to compute expected lifespan)
  • Family members: names, relations, ages you enter (used locally to generate scenarios)
  • Daily check-ins: green/yellow/red selections with optional detail flags

All of the above is stored locally using Apple's SwiftData framework. None of it leaves your device.

3. What the App Does NOT Do

  • No account creation
  • No cloud sync (no CloudKit, no Google, no Firebase, no proprietary server)
  • No third-party analytics SDKs
  • No advertising frameworks
  • No user tracking across apps or websites
  • No HealthKit data access
  • No Photos / Camera / Contacts / Calendar access
  • No network requests of any kind

4. Notifications

The Toll uses iOS local notifications only — the system delivers a daily reminder you scheduled in Settings. No remote push notifications. Permission can be revoked anytime via iOS Settings → Notifications → The Toll.

5. Children's Privacy

The Toll is rated 4+ and not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any data from anyone, regardless of age.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all data lives on your device, you control retention completely. Uninstalling the app deletes all data. You can also delete individual check-ins or family members within the app at any time.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, the new version will be posted here with an updated Effective Date. Material changes may also be highlighted in the app.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email: liujinxingde2008@hotmail.com
