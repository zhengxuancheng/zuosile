Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data private? Does the app upload anything?

All data stays on your device. The Toll has no servers, no accounts, and no internet connection. Nothing is ever uploaded. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

How accurate is the Damage Score?

The Damage Score is a motivational lifestyle indicator inspired by actuarial research on lifestyle risk factors (including work by Spiegelhalter, 2012 BMJ). It is not a medical device and does not predict your actual lifespan. Individual results vary significantly. Please consult a healthcare professional for medical advice.

I bought the app but the purchase isn't restoring.

Go to Settings → Restore Purchase inside the app. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase. If issues persist, contact us below.

Can I add more than one family member?

Yes — the Family tab lets you add as many family members as you like. Each person gets their own impact timeline based on your check-in history.

How do I delete all my data?

Go to Settings inside the app and use the Reset function. Or simply delete the app — all data will be removed from your device along with it.

What iOS version is required?

The Toll requires iOS 17.0 or later on iPhone.

我可以把界面切换成中文吗？

可以。App 会自动跟随您 iPhone 的系统语言（支持简体中文 / 繁体中文 / English）。在 iPhone 设置 → 通用 → 语言与地区 切换系统语言即可。
