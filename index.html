FAQ

Is this medical advice? No. The Toll uses imagined family scenarios to motivate behavioral change. It is not medical advice and does not replace a doctor's evaluation.

Is my data sent to a server? No. All data lives on your device. There is no account, no cloud sync, no third-party tracking.

Why does the app show family loss imagery? The Toll is built on a single insight: self-warnings don't work; family guilt does. The scenarios are imagined consequences, not predictions.

Can I turn off the family scenarios? Yes. Open Settings inside the app and disable scenario rotation, or simply add no family members.
